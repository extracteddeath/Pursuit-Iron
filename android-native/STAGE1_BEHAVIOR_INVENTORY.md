Require explicit evidence for both generated and custom variants, and both coaching-off and coaching-on where the behavior differs. Never compare by copying a known incorrect web output. Device-only acceptance belongs to S8; no APK before S7. S1 closes inventory/assignment, not those individual tests. Record exact test methods, paired result, accepted independent expectation and source SHA as stages execute.
