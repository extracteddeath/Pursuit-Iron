# Serratus and tibialis native catalog review

Two existing web identities now have independent, provisional native muscle ledgers:

| Exact source identity | Native direct target | Loading convention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `serratus-pushup` | `SERRATUS_ANTERIOR` | Unweighted |
| `tibialis-raise` | `TIBIALIS_ANTERIOR` | Unweighted, confirmed wall support |

The actual web source preserves `legacySubregion=serratus` under broad `core`,
and `legacySubregion=tibialis` under broad `calves`. The review registry pins
those exact subregions as well as the existing identity/equipment/rep source
facts, rejecting anatomical drift or a return to broad abs/calf counting.
The native definitions retain those source identities and practical rep bands,
but do not copy the broad labels into abdominal or plantar-flexion calf credit.

Primary research used for anatomical/function review:

- [Push-up-plus phases and scapular muscle activity, 13 healthy participants](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4968519/).
- [Tibialis-anterior EMG/sonomyography during dorsiflexion, 27 healthy participants](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3925007/).

These studies inform the decision to separate those functions. They do not
validate the native exercise's numeric set credit, personal MEV/MAV/MRV,
training response, clinical effect or program quality. One direct set remains
an explicitly provisional accounting convention; no EMG-to-set conversion is
made. No new fractional credits are inferred for abs, calves or other groups.
The tibialis definition is unweighted and wall-supported, requiring the exact
athlete-confirmed `wall-support` fixture. Bodyweight sets have no external
load; 0 is not substituted for a missing physical-load grant. Cable serratus,
banded tibialis, weighted and timed variants remain separate work.

New first-program policy v11 declares all 22 groups. Saved v10 keeps its 20
rows, v9 its 18, and v1-v8 their 16, including original order and decimal
representation. The custom-policy editor retains that saved scope; adding
current targets is explicit and requires complete user-entered values. Partial
scopes fail closed. Existing exercise credits are unchanged.

Acceptance requires real generation, every converted cycle week, native
approval/issuance/logging/correction/Finish/History/cold replay, and independent
pre-change byte compatibility. Full catalog/engine parity, coaching superiority,
physical-device and app-wide UI acceptance are not established by this review.
