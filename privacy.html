Privacy Policy

Pursuit Iron · Effective 21 October 2026

Short version: Pursuit Iron has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your programs, workouts and body measurements are stored only on your device. Nobody, including the developer, can see them unless you choose to share or export them.

What the app stores

To build and adjust your training, the app keeps the following in your device's local browser storage:

  • Training programs, cycles and exercise preferences (including banned exercises and equipment).
  • Workout history: exercises, weights, reps, effort ratings, notes and dates.
  • Profile details you enter, such as bodyweight, body measurements, sex and birth date, which are used for strength standards.
  • App settings such as theme, units, rest-timer and reminder preferences, plus profile-card choices, an optional display name and favorite charts.

This data is processed locally. It is not automatically transmitted to the developer or to third parties; you can choose to export or share it.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-in or account, and no transmission of your profile name or access to your contacts.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or telemetry services.
  • No location, camera, microphone or health-platform access.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Network use

  • The app's own files are downloaded from its host (GitHub Pages) when you install or update it. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The program gallery is downloaded from the same host. Your saved programs and workout records are not included in that request.
  • Some buttons open external sites only when you tap them: exercise videos (YouTube search), program-gallery submission (GitHub), support (Ko-fi) and bug reports (your email app). Those sites' own privacy policies apply.

Notifications

If you turn on training reminders or rest-timer alerts, they are scheduled and shown on your device. No notification content is sent to a server.

Backups and sharing

You control exports. Backup files and share cards are created on your device and go only where you send them, such as your files, cloud drive or messaging app.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Training data → Reset everything in the app (tap twice to confirm) to clear training records and body-profile data. Device preferences remain. To remove all locally stored data and preferences, clear this site's data in the browser that runs the app. Uninstalling an Android web wrapper may leave browser site data behind. Data remains on the device until deleted or cleared by the browser; storage protection does not replace a separate backup. Delete exported files separately wherever you saved or shared them.

Children

Pursuit Iron is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds optional accounts or cloud sync, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, describing exactly what is stored, where, and how to delete it. The effective date above will change with any update.
